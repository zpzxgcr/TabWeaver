# Privacy Policy for TabWeaver (TabWeaver 隐私政策)

**Last updated / 最近更新日期:** 2026-11-19

Welcome to TabWeaver! We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to transparency regarding what data is accessed, processed, or stored when you use the TabWeaver Chrome Extension ("the Extension").

欢迎使用 TabWeaver！我们高度重视您的隐私。本隐私政策旨在向您说明使用 TabWeaver Chrome 扩展程序（以下简称“本扩展”）时，我们如何处理数据与保护您的个人隐私。

---

## 1. Summary & Core Principles (核心原则)

- **100% Client-Side Execution (纯本地客户端运行):** TabWeaver runs entirely in your local browser sandbox. We do **NOT** operate any external servers, backend APIs, or databases to collect your data.
- **No Personal Data Collection (不收集任何个人数据):** We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), browsing history, account passwords, cookies, or payment information.
- **User-Controlled Data Flow (用户自主控制数据流向):** Webpage content is extracted **only** when you explicitly select a target tab (using `#` or `@`) and send it to your current active AI conversation.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking (无第三方追踪或埋点):** The Extension does not integrate any third-party telemetry, tracking pixels, or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics).

---

## 2. Information We Access and How It Is Used (权限与数据访问说明)

TabWeaver requests only the minimum permissions necessary to deliver its core functionality:

### A. Tab Management (`tabs`, `activeTab`)
- **Purpose:** Used to list open browser tabs within the `#` / `@` popup picker, allowing you to select which target tab you wish to inspect or control, and to switch between AI conversations and target tabs.
- **Data Handling:** Tab metadata (titles, URLs, IDs) is processed purely in local memory and is never logged or sent to any remote server.

### B. In-Page Scripting & DOM Extraction (`scripting`, `<all_urls>`)
- **Purpose:** When you instruct an AI to interact with a target webpage, the Extension injects a lightweight extractor (`dom-distiller.js`) to capture an accessible snapshot of the target page's readable content, and executes the specific DOM operations (clicking buttons, filling inputs, scrolling) generated by your AI.
- **Data Handling:** Content is extracted only upon explicit user command and is routed solely into your active AI web interface (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek). It is never sent to the Extension's developers or external parties.
- **Why `<all_urls>`:** Users can freely choose to automate and inspect any web page of their choice (documentation, news, e-commerce, research sites). Because target sites cannot be predetermined, broad host permissions are strictly necessary for the user-directed automation.

### C. Local Storage (`storage`)
- **Purpose:** Used to save your UI preferences (language selection, theme mode, platform matrix visibility) and optional local diagnostics in `chrome.storage.local`.
- **Data Handling:** All settings remain local to your browser profile. Local diagnostic logs (if explicitly enabled) are kept strictly within your local machine and automatically redact sensitive tokens, passwords, and authorization headers.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure (数据共享与第三方披露)

- **We DO NOT sell, rent, trade, or transfer your data to outside parties.**
- The data extracted from target tabs is transmitted exclusively into the AI chat interface that **you** are logged into and interacting with, based on your direct prompt. Your interactions with third-party AI platforms (OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek, Anthropic, etc.) are governed by their respective terms of service and privacy policies.

---

## 4. Security & Compliance (安全性与合规承诺)

- **No Remote Code Execution:** TabWeaver does not use `eval()`, `new Function()`, or load external scripts from CDNs. All scripts are packaged locally within the extension conforming to Chrome Web Store Manifest V3 guidelines.
- **Chrome Web Store Policy Compliance:** TabWeaver strictly complies with the [Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/), including the Limited Use Requirements.
